# 06. PATCH: Official Brand Kit, Expensive Diamond, Recent Work First

Apply this on top of files 00 to 05. Where this patch disagrees with an earlier file, this patch wins.

Why this patch exists, from the client's feedback:
1. The site is liked, but tech and recent projects do not get enough emphasis.
2. The diamond does not look expensive.
3. The client sent an official brand kit (Behance) and said to use it. It uses pale green, pale blue, black and white, not the gold and ice blue used so far.

### Fonts (from the type board)

### Logo set (from the logo board)

2. Wordmark THE FUNDRAISER.
3. Circular badge: face in the center, THE FUNDRAISER written twice around it, with dot separators.
4. Horizontal lockup: face on the left, THE and FUNDRAISER stacked on the right.
