## 0. Loader

Purpose: set the tone in under two seconds, and hide asset loading.

1. Black screen. A rough, grey indigo stone fades in at center, outline only.
2. A thin line of light cuts across it from top left to bottom right. Sound cue if audio is on: a glass tick.
3. The stone splits into facets. Each facet flies out two pixels and settles into the master diamond shape.
4. The diamond fills with ice light, flashes white, and the page reveals through a diamond shaped mask that expands to full screen.
5. Returning visitors in the same session skip it.
6. Hard limit: 1.8 seconds. If assets are slow, the loader keeps cutting until ready, but never loops more than twice.

## 1. Hero

Purpose: make the visitor say "okay, this is different" and tell them what this is in five words.

Layout, desktop: headline left on a two column grid, hero diamond right, large and slightly overlapping the headline. Void glow behind it.
Layout, mobile: diamond on top at 60 percent of the screen width, headline below, buttons pinned at the bottom of the first screen.

Copy:
1. Mono eyebrow: WEB3 · DEFI · GROWTH STRATEGY
2. Headline: "Pressure makes diamonds."
3. Subline: "The Fundraiser turns small Web3 projects into names people trust. Raids, communities, visibility and builds. All proven in public."
4. Primary button: "Watch the cut" (scrolls to Chapter 01).
5. Secondary button: "Work with us" (opens Telegram @Senor_Izyche).
6. Micro line under buttons in mono: "Scroll to cut the stone."
7. Small corner badge: the face logo, the handle @IZYCHE_.

Motion:
1. Headline reveals word by word, 80 milliseconds apart, with the diamond sheen sweeping across "diamonds" once.
2. The diamond rotates slowly on its own, and tilts toward the cursor on desktop or with device tilt on mobile.
3. Two or three mascot peeks later in the page, never in the hero.
